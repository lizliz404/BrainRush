## GEO blocker (highest leverage, zero ongoing maintenance)

Raw HTML body is `<div id="root"></div>`. A JS-less crawler sees **1 visible
character**. GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot / OAI-SearchBot do not execute
JavaScript, so nothing in the app (modes, mistake book, local scores, maker)
can be cited from the page itself. `llms.txt` alone does not compensate.

## SEO ceiling

One indexable URL. A single page can realistically compete only for the brand
plus a few generic 口算 / 单词 terms.

### Reading

- The traffic is **pep-words vocabulary search in Chinese**, essentially all of
  it. The 537-page structure is doing its job on Bing.
- The game earns no category demand at all: **zero** 口算 / 数学 / 算题 queries in
  the entire report. It gets ~53 brand-cluster impressions and 1 click, and
  most of those impressions are a different entity — "brain rush tv show",
  "what is baldwin brain rush", "microfest medical the brain rush",
  "lomba brain rush", a Toss mini-app hosted at `brainrush.private-apps.tossmini.com`.
- `初中英语单词` at 2.4% CTR looks like a snippet problem but is not: position 8.4
  is where ~2-3% CTR is normal, and the sibling query at position 7.4 does 5.5%.
  The lever there is position, not copy.
- Do not claim a CTR fix would add ~23 clicks; that assumed an 8% CTR at
  position 8, which does not happen.

### Decision

No further page expansion, and no title rewrite for the game. The honest
reading is that `brainrush.run` is not a search asset: there is no query demand
for a Chinese mental-math game on Bing under this name, and the brand queries
that do arrive mostly mean a different product. Pep-words is the working asset,
and its remaining lever is authority/position, not on-page text.
