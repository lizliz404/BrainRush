`package-lock.json` carried 34 `resolved` URLs pointing at `mirrors.tencentyun.com`
(Tencent-internal, public DNS fails). Those entries arrived with the Tailwind
deps in `496cdb1b` (2026-08-03). Every deploy from that commit on failed at
`npm ci`, so live stayed on `03a7dbcc` (2026-05-26) and the 2026-08-07/08 SEO
work was never served.

Fixed in `f815a55`: 34 URLs → `registry.npmjs.org`, plus `.npmrc` pinning the
registry so a future lockfile cannot re-bake a private mirror. Deploy succeeded
and live now serves the Aug 8 state (`/llms.txt` = `text/plain`, sitemap
`lastmod` present, updated meta description).

**Consequence for the numbers below: they measure the May 26 build, not the

## GEO blocker (highest leverage, zero ongoing maintenance)

Raw HTML body is `<div id="root"></div>`. A JS-less crawler sees **1 visible
character**. GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot / OAI-SearchBot do not execute
JavaScript, so nothing in the app (modes, mistake book, local scores, maker)
can be cited from the page itself. `llms.txt` alone does not compensate.

## SEO ceiling

One indexable URL. A single page can realistically compete only for the brand
plus a few generic 口算 / 单词 terms.
