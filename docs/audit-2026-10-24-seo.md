## GEO blocker (highest leverage, zero ongoing maintenance)

Raw HTML body is `<div id="root"></div>`. A JS-less crawler sees **1 visible
character**. GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot / OAI-SearchBot do not execute
JavaScript, so nothing in the app (modes, mistake book, local scores, maker)
can be cited from the page itself. `llms.txt` alone does not compensate.

## SEO ceiling

One indexable URL. A single page can realistically compete only for the brand
plus a few generic 口算 / 单词 terms.

## www -> apex: why it never worked, and the fix

1. `public/_redirects` — not applicable, Pages has no domain-level redirects.
2. A zone rule — impossible at the time: every DNS record in the zone was
   **DNS-only (grey cloud)**, so requests went straight to
   `brainrush-gh.pages.dev` and never reached Cloudflare's edge.
3. Fixed by making `www.brainrush.run` proxied and adding a zone Page Rule
   `www.brainrush.run/*` -> `https://brainrush.run/$1`, 301.

Verified 2026-10-24: `https://www.brainrush.run/`, `http://www.brainrush.run/`,
`/llms.txt`, and `/sitemap.xml` all 301 to the apex equivalent in one hop, TLS
valid; apex and `pep-words.brainrush.run` still 200.

The Page Rule is legacy Cloudflare config. A Single Redirect rule in the zone
is the modern equivalent if this ever needs touching again.

## Where this leaves SEO

Technical surface is done: page is crawlable without JavaScript, canonical on
apex, single honest sitemap URL, x-default hreflang only, llms.txt factual,
www consolidated, deploys working again.

Organic search is not the acquisition channel for this product at its current
authority: 2 estimated visits/month, brand-only rankings, and a 537-page
control group that produces nothing. Further on-page work is not the lever;
external links or the places parents actually search would be a different
